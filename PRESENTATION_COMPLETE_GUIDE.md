## 🎨 DESIGN INSPIRATION

### Color Scheme (Professional Tech):
- Primary: Deep Blue (#003366)
- Secondary: Green (#00AA44)
- Accent: Orange (#FF6600)
- Neutral: Gray (#333333)

### Font Choices:
- Titles: Montserrat Bold or Calibri Bold
- Body: Calibri or Arial
- Code: Courier New
- Never: Comic Sans, decorative fonts

### Layout Best Practices:
- Left-aligned text (easier to read)
- Images on right (visual balance)
- Max 5 bullet points per slide
- White space is good
- Avoid clutter

## 💡 BONUS TIPS

### To Impress Your Professor:

1. **Start Strong**
   - "Good morning. Thank you for having me."
   - Make eye contact
   - Speak with confidence

2. **Show Passion**
   - Explain WHY you chose this project
   - Share challenges you overcame
   - Mention impact on real users (autistic children)

3. **Be Technical**
   - Use industry terminology correctly
   - Explain decisions (why Kubernetes, not Docker Compose?)
   - Show you understand trade-offs

4. **Handle Questions Well**
   - Listen carefully
   - Think before answering
   - "Great question..." or "That's a good point..."
   - Admit if you don't know ("I'll research that")

5. **Close Strong**
   - "Thank you for your time"
   - "Any final questions?"
   - Smile
